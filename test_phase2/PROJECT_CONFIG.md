# Community Education Access Project

## Overview
This project aims to improve access to quality secondary education for youth in the Riverside Community through participatory methods and community-led solutions.

## Project Context

### Background
The Riverside Community has identified education access as a critical need. Youth in the community face multiple barriers to accessing secondary education, including distance, cost, and infrastructure challenges.

### Goals
- Identify and address barriers to secondary education access
- Develop community-led solutions
- Build capacity within the community
- Create sustainable improvements

## Configuration

### Scope
- **Geographic**: Riverside Community and surrounding areas
- **Thematic**: Secondary education access
- **Population**: Youth aged 12-18 and their families
- **Duration**: 6 months (January - June 2024)
