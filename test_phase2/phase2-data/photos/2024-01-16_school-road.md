# Road to Riverside Secondary School

![Road to Riverside Secondary School](../../assets/photos/2024-01-16_school-road.jpg)

*Photo: Road to Riverside Secondary School - January 16, 2024*

## Description
Photo of the main road connecting Riverside Community to Riverside Secondary School, taken during an observation walk.

## Observations

### Road Characteristics
- **Type**: Unpaved dirt road
- **Width**: ~4-5 meters
- **Surface**: Dirt with some gravel sections
- **Condition**: Poor, with many potholes and ruts
- **Drainage**: Limited, prone to flooding

### Distance Markers
- **Starting Point**: Riverside Community Center (0km)
- **Current Location**: ~3.5km from community center
- **School Location**: ~7km from community center
- **Total Distance**: 7km one way, 14km round trip

### Surface Conditions
- **Dry Season**: Dusty, bumpy, difficult for walking
- **Rainy Season**: Muddy, slippery, often impassable
- **Potholes**: Numerous, some filled with water
- **Ruts**: Deep ruts from vehicle traffic

### Traffic
- **Vehicles**: Occasional cars, motorcycles, bicycles
- **Pedestrians**: Youth walking to/from school, community members
- **Animals**: Cows, goats, chickens often on the road
- **Frequency**: Light traffic, mostly during school hours

### Safety Concerns
- **Visibility**: Poor visibility around curves and hills
- **Lighting**: No street lights, dark at night
- **Wild Animals**: Occasional reports of snakes, especially in tall grass
- **Crime**: Some reports of theft, especially of bicycles
- **Harassment**: Youth (especially girls) report harassment from motorcyclists

### Infrastructure Along the Road
- **Bridges**: 2 small wooden bridges over streams
- **Drainage**: Some drainage ditches, but often clogged
- **Signage**: No signs indicating distance or direction
- **Rest Areas**: No formal rest areas, some trees for shade
- **Water Points**: No water points along the route

## Context
This photo was taken on January 16, 2024, during an observation walk to document the route that youth take to get to school. The walk was part of Phase 2 data collection for the Education Access Project.

## Journey Details
- **Start Time**: 08:30 from Riverside Community
- **Photo Time**: 09:00 (3.5km from start)
- **End Time**: 10:15 at Riverside Secondary School
- **Total Time**: 1 hour 45 minutes one way
- **Walking Speed**: ~4 km/h (slow due to road conditions)

## Youth Experiences
Based on discussions with youth who use this road daily:

### Daily Journey
- **Departure**: 06:00 from home
- **Arrival at School**: 07:45-08:00
- **School Ends**: 15:00
- **Arrival at Home**: 16:45-17:00
- **Total Time**: ~3.5 hours daily (7 hours round trip)

### Challenges Faced
- **Fatigue**: Arrive at school already tired
- **Time**: Less time for homework and study
- **Safety**: Concerns about safety, especially in the dark
- **Weather**: Road becomes impassable in heavy rain
- **Cost**: Some pay for motorcycle taxis when road is bad

### Coping Strategies
- **Walking Groups**: Youth walk together for safety
- **Bicycles**: Some have bicycles (but road is rough)
- **Shared Transportation**: Occasional shared cars
- **Boarding**: Some stay with relatives near school

## Seasonal Variations

### Dry Season (November - April)
- **Condition**: Dusty, bumpy
- **Travel Time**: ~1.5-2 hours one way
- **Main Challenges**: Dust, heat, fatigue

### Rainy Season (May - October)
- **Condition**: Muddy, slippery, flooded sections
- **Travel Time**: 2-3 hours one way (or impossible)
- **Main Challenges**: Mud, flooding, safety
- **Missed Days**: Youth often miss school during heavy rains

## Potential Solutions

### Short-Term (Immediate)
1. **Walking Groups**: Organize and formalize walking groups
2. **Safety Measures**: Provide reflective vests, whistles
3. **Maintenance**: Community road maintenance days
4. **Awareness**: Educate drivers to watch for pedestrians

### Medium-Term (1-2 years)
1. **Road Grading**: Improve road surface and drainage
2. **Bicycle Program**: Provide bicycles to youth
3. **Transportation**: Organize community transportation
4. **Lighting**: Install solar-powered lights at key points

### Long-Term (2+ years)
1. **Paving**: Pave the road (expensive but ideal)
2. **Boarding**: Build boarding facilities at school
3. **New School**: Advocate for a school in the community
