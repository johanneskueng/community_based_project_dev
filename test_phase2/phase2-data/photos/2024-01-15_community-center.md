# Riverside Community Center Main Hall

![Riverside Community Center Main Hall](../../assets/photos/2024-01-15_community-center.jpg)

*Photo: Riverside Community Center Main Hall - January 15, 2024*

## Description
Photo of the main hall at the Riverside Community Center where community meetings and education activities are held.

## Observations

### Physical Characteristics
- **Size**: Approximately 10m x 12m (can accommodate ~50 people)
- **Shape**: Rectangular
- **Ceiling Height**: ~4m
- **Flooring**: Concrete, in good condition
- **Walls**: Painted concrete blocks, light blue color

### Furniture and Equipment
- **Seating**: 40 plastic chairs, stackable
- **Tables**: 5 rectangular tables (1.5m x 0.8m)
- **Whiteboard**: Large whiteboard on north wall (2m x 1m)
- **Storage**: Cupboard on east wall for supplies
- **Lighting**: 8 fluorescent lights, evenly distributed
- **Ventilation**: 4 windows (2 on each side), 1 ceiling fan

### Accessibility Features
- **Entrance**: Wheelchair-accessible ramp at main entrance
- **Pathways**: Clear pathways between chairs and tables
- **Bathrooms**: Accessible bathroom nearby
- **Seating**: Some chairs have armrests for support

### Condition
- **Overall**: Good condition, well-maintained
- **Cleanliness**: Clean, well-kept
- **Maintenance**: Recent paint, no visible damage
- **Comfort**: Adequate for meetings, could use more ventilation

## Context
This photo was taken on January 15, 2024, during the first community meeting for the Education Access Project. The space was set up for approximately 25 participants, with chairs arranged in a circle to facilitate discussion.

## Usage Notes
- **Capacity**: Comfortably seats 40-50 people
- **Acoustics**: Good for discussions, but can be echoey with large groups
- **Natural Light**: Good natural light from windows
- **Power**: Multiple electrical outlets available
- **Internet**: No WiFi available in this space

## Community Significance
- This is the primary meeting space for the Riverside Community
- Used for community meetings, education activities, social events
- Managed by the Riverside Community Association
- Available for community use free of charge

## Potential for Project
- **Strengths**: Central location, good size, accessible, well-equipped
- **Opportunities**: Could be used for study groups, workshops, meetings
- **Challenges**: Limited storage space, no projector/screen
- **Recommendations**: Add more ventilation, consider portable projector
